Eight demo apps for seven lectures

Games and toys built to be projected in class and played afterwards: program a CPU, decompile machine code, wire a network, solve a database mystery, teach a neural network, break a bot, and catch hallucinated references. Each one is a simulation that runs entirely in your browser and ties back to a chapter.

Chapter 7 · puzzle · assembly · simulation

Program the CPU

Solve puzzles on the toy Von Neumann computer by writing assembly, then watch every fetch, decode and execute step. Earn stars for correct, small and fast programs.

 Chapter 7 · workbench · assembly · sandbox

Von Neumann Workbench

Write your own assembly in a real editor, pick from 70+ examples, and customize the toy computer: memory size, extended instructions, memory speed and cell width. Watch every fetch, decode and execute step travel along the arrows.

 Chapter 8 · puzzle · compiler · reverse-engineering

Decompile Me

Solve machine-code cold cases: read the toy-machine assembly of a hidden C++ program, write code that behaves the same, and watch the compiler stages and an assembly diff judge you.

 Chapter 9 · game · simulation · networking

Network Builder

Wire up dorm and office networks with switches, routers, firewalls and Wi-Fi, then watch packets travel hop by hop. The simulator explains exactly why a ping worked or failed.

 Chapter 10 · mystery · SQL · puzzle

The Missing Trophy

The club trophy vanished from a locked room, and the only witnesses are database tables. Solve the case with SELECT, JOIN, GROUP BY, subqueries and an index.

 Chapter 11 · simulation · machine learning · challenge

Draw & Teach a Brain

Paint coloured dots, build a tiny neural network and watch it learn the boundary live, then freeze it and ask about new dots. Explore overfitting, a learning rate that blows up and biased data.

 Chapter 12 · game · security · simulation

Break the Bot

Trick seven simulated, rule-based assistants into giving up a harmless codeword, then read the post-mortem and switch to Defender mode to balance security against usability.

 Chapter 13 · game · citation · verification

Real or Hallucinated?

An AI assistant hands you a bibliography. Judge each reference as real, fake or real-with-wrong-details, spend time on DOI and author checks, and keep your essay credible.
